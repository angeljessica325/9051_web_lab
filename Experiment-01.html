Welcome to Ooty

Ooty is one of the most beautiful hill stations in India. It is famous for its green mountains, beautiful gardens and pleasant weather.

About Ooty

Ooty is located in Tamil Nadu. It is also known as the Queen of Hill Stations. Many tourists visit this place to enjoy its natural beauty.

Why I Like Ooty

  • Beautiful mountains
  • Cool climate
  • Tea gardens
  • Peaceful environment

Famous Tourist Places

  1. Ooty Lake
  2. Botanical Garden
  3. Doddabetta Peak
  4. Rose Garden
